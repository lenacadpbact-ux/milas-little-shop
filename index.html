Adventures of Mila

Mila's story follows a young girl learning what home really means — through family, friendship, love, and belonging. She moves to a new country, makes new friends, and works through the loss of her beloved dogs, slowly finding her way back to loving again. As her journey grows, so does her world — she explores new cities, famous landmarks, national parks, and the wonders of nature along the way.
